# Evidence-Led Demo Script

## Opening

This demo is not trying to prove that AI can read documents. It is showing how document intake can be controlled before AI use.

## Demo sequence

1. Show the sample document entering the workflow
2. Show extraction and metadata capture
3. Show sensitivity classification
4. Show detected PII or confidential content
5. Show the redacted working copy
6. Show the human approval gate
7. Show approved redacted content being used
8. Show the audit summary

## Narration points

- The important control is not the model answer; it is the boundary before the model.
- Raw material is not automatically treated as safe input.
- Approval state controls downstream AI use.
- The audit trail records what happened and why.

## Close

The proof is simple: we can see what entered, what was detected, what was blocked, what was approved, and what evidence remains.
