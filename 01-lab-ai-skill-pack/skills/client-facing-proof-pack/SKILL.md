# Client-Facing Proof Pack Skill

## Purpose

Turn technical workflow work into client-facing proof: clear explanation, evidence, screenshots or logs where available, risks, limitations, and next actions.

## Use this skill when

- A demo needs to be explained to a client
- A workflow needs a proof artefact
- Technical notes need conversion into a business-facing summary
- Safe Intake needs visible evidence of controls

## Proof pack structure

1. Plain-English summary
2. Workflow map
3. What was tested
4. What was blocked
5. What evidence was preserved
6. Human review points
7. Known limitations
8. Next decision

## Required tone

Direct, practical, evidence-first. No hype. No claims beyond what the artefact proves.

## Output contract

Return:

- client-ready summary
- proof map
- evidence table
- risk and limitation notes
- next action
