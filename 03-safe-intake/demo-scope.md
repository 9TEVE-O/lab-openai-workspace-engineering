# Safe Intake Demo Scope

## Purpose

Define the smallest useful Safe Intake demo.

## Demo goal

Show that a document can move through controlled intake before AI use.

## Included in demo

- Upload or paste sample document
- Extract visible text
- Classify sensitivity
- Detect obvious PII/confidential content
- Show redacted working copy
- Require human approval
- Allow approved redacted content only
- Generate simple audit summary

## Excluded from demo

- Legal compliance certification
- Production security claims
- Full enterprise access control
- Automated legal advice
- Irreversible external actions
- Raw document ingestion into AI

## Success criterion

A viewer can see what entered, what was detected, what was blocked, what was approved, and what evidence was preserved.
