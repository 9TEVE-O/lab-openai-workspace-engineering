# AI Asset Activation Operator — AAO-001 v0.1

## Status

Approved with conditions for controlled internal use.

## Purpose

Provide a bounded control method for deciding whether dormant, completed or previously created AI work should be activated, held, archived or left unchanged.

The operator exists to prevent storage from being mistaken for active authority and to stop completed work being reactivated merely because it is discoverable.

## Core control

For each candidate asset, determine its current status before use:

- active;
- paused;
- completed capability;
- archived reference;
- held;
- stale;
- not canon;
- irrelevant to the current objective.

Activation must have a concrete consequence. Retrieval alone is not activation.

## Authority boundaries

AAO-001 does not have authority to:

- create independent public or commercial claims;
- approve consequential releases;
- convert an unvalidated draft into approved canon;
- infer approval from repetition, storage or implementation;
- represent itself as a native Notion AI skill.

Consequential release decisions remain human-owned.

## Change control

Material changes to the operator require retesting and reapproval before the revised behaviour is treated as approved.

## Evidence boundary

This repository record preserves the approved internal-use status and operating boundaries. It does not claim external validation, production readiness or independent commercial validation.
